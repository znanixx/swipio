Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Tech Interview Practice free?

    Tech Interview Practice is free to download with core features and a daily practice limit. Optional premium unlocks unlimited learning, all themes, ad-free experience, and more through in-app purchases.

  • How do I restore my subscription?

    Your subscription is tied to your App Store or Google Play account. After reinstalling the app or switching to a new device, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe — your subscription will be restored automatically.

  • Can I transfer my subscription from iOS to Android (or the other way)?

    No. Subscriptions are tied to either the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and don't transfer between platforms. You would need to subscribe again on the new OS. On a new device with the same platform, sign in with the same account and your subscription will be restored automatically.

  • Notifications don't work or arrive at the wrong time

    First, ensure notifications are enabled in the app's Settings. Then check your device's battery or power-saving settings — aggressive power-saving modes can block or delay local push notifications.

  • Does Tech Interview Practice work offline?

    Yes. All questions are stored locally on your device. You can practice offline anytime — no internet connection required after the initial setup.
